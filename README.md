**Domain:** Retail & E-Commerce Analytics  
**Project Type:** End-to-End Data Analytics

---

## Project Overview

Retail Sales Performance & Customer Insights is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing retail sales data and identifying meaningful business insights.

This project follows a complete analytics workflow using **Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI**. It covers data cleaning, business analysis, exploratory data analysis, customer segmentation, churn-risk analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

The main objective is to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, and business trends through data-driven insights.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Evaluate store and salesperson performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Segment customers based on purchasing patterns.
- Identify customers with potential churn risk.
- Analyze campaign performance and sales trends.
- Support business decisions through data insights.

## Project Workflow

### Stage 1 – Excel: Data Cleaning & Analysis
- Imported and organized datasets.
- Checked data types, missing values, and duplicates.
- Validated keys and data consistency.
- Performed initial data analysis.
- Prepared datasets for further analysis.

### Stage 2 – SQL: Business Analysis
- Worked with the retail database using TiDB Cloud.
- Applied filtering, sorting, grouping, and aggregate functions.
- Practiced joins, subqueries, CTEs, and window functions.
- Developed queries to analyze sales, customers, products, and store performance.

### Stage 3 – Python: Exploratory Data Analysis & Customer Analytics
- Loaded and validated datasets using Pandas.
- Performed data preprocessing and EDA.
- Created new features for sales and customer analysis.
- Analyzed customer purchasing behaviour.
- Applied customer segmentation and churn-risk analysis.
- Created visualizations to identify business patterns.

**Customer Segments Identified**
- Champions
- Loyal Regulars
- Occasional Buyers
- Lapsing Buyers

### Stage 4 – Power BI: Dashboard & Visualization
- Developed interactive dashboards.
- Created key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Analyzed revenue and sales performance.
- Visualized business performance across different categories.
- Used interactive filters and visuals for reporting.
